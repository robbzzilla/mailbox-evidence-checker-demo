# Mailbox migration evidence review

Get a written check of one Google Workspace to Microsoft 365 migration batch for **$250 USD fixed**. The review covers up to five mailboxes and includes one written recheck.

## What you receive

- An evidence table comparing mapped folder counts, reported skips and expected alias destinations.
- A list of differences and evidence gaps. Each finding names the affected redacted record and the next check for your administrator.
- One written recheck of corrected or newly available evidence for the same batch.

Delivery takes two business days after written scope approval and receipt of the complete agreed inputs. The review is AI-assisted. We agree on permitted tools and evidence handling in writing before you share files. Your administrator owns the migration, any live delivery checks and any changes to mail settings.

## Check whether your batch is ready

Start with the migration window and time zone, the number of mailboxes, and which reports your administrator can export. A comparison needs source and destination counts for the same cutoff, the approved folder map and exclusions, and any available skip and alias records. Use anonymous references in place of mailbox addresses. [See the fictional sample and export fields](sample-review.md#check-your-export-fields).
